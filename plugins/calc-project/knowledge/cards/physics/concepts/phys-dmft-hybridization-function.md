## Core statement

DMFT 杂化函数（DMFT hybridization function）的核心陈述是：The DMFT hybridization function is the dynamical coupling that encodes particle exchange between the interacting site and its effective bath and thereby defines the impurity's effective bare Green function.

## Definition and distinctions

- **定义：** The hybridization function Delta(omega) is a frequency-dependent, generally matrix-valued embedding quantity that represents propagation from the interacting subspace into the bath and back.
- **形式化表达：**
  - **effective bare green function：** G_0(omega) = [omega - epsilon_0 - Delta(omega)]^(-1)
  - **cavity construction：** Delta(omega) = sum_ij t_0i G^-_ij(omega) t_j0
- **关键区分：**
  - **bath：** The bath is the effective surrounding system; Delta(omega) is the dynamical influence of that bath on the interacting subspace, not the bath itself.
  - **impurity self energy：** Delta(omega) excludes the interaction acting inside the impurity, whereas Sigma_imp(omega) is generated by solving that interacting impurity problem.
- **物理含义：** Its real-frequency structure controls how impurity states exchange particles and broaden through the environment; on Matsubara frequencies it is the solver input that carries the dynamical embedding.
- **边界：** Delta(omega) depends on the chosen correlated subspace and the self-consistent lattice environment; treating it as an arbitrary broadening parameter loses the DMFT embedding meaning.
