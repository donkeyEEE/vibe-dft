## Core statement

连续时间杂化展开杂质求解器（continuous-time hybridization-expansion impurity solver）的核心陈述是：Continuous-time hybridization-expansion quantum Monte Carlo (CT-HYB) treats the local interacting Hamiltonian nonperturbatively and samples electron creation and annihilation events generated by coupling to a continuous bath.

## Formulation

- **展开：** Expand the partition function in Delta; each configuration is a time-ordered sequence of impurity creation and annihilation operators, while all bath contractions at a given order combine into hybridization determinants.
- **局域迹：** The remaining weight is a trace of the operator sequence over local many-body states, so general Coulomb matrices, spin flips, pair hopping, crystal fields, and spin-orbit terms can be retained when the implementation supports them.
- **why it suits strong coupling：** The atomic interaction is included in the unexpanded local problem and only impurity-bath exchange is expanded, making CT-HYB especially natural near atomic and strong-coupling regimes.
- **计算代价：** Typical order grows roughly with beta and hybridization strength; determinant work grows polynomially with order, but evaluation of the local trace can grow exponentially with the number of spin-orbitals unless symmetries and block structure reduce it.
- **符号问题边界：** Density-density, symmetry-separated cases can be sign-benign, but off-diagonal hybridization, general interactions, clusters, or spin-orbit coupling may introduce a sign problem; absence of a sign problem is not universal to CT-HYB.
- **输出边界：** Results are statistically exact for the specified action under ergodic sampling, but remain imaginary-time data and inherit low-temperature, autocorrelation, measurement, and continuation limits.

公式中的频率、能量、相互作用强度、温度和带宽默认采用一致的能量单位；若取自然单位，则令 $\hbar=k_{\mathrm B}=1$。矩阵指标指向卡片所声明的局域轨道、团簇站点或动量扇区，具体符号约定以 Formulation 为准。
