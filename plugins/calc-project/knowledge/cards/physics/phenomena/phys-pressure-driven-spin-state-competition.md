## Core statement

压力驱动的自旋态竞争（pressure-driven spin-state competition）的核心陈述是：Pressure-driven spin-state transitions in multiorbital correlated materials arise from a changing balance among crystal-field splitting, Hund exchange, bandwidth, and Coulomb repulsion and may therefore coincide with—but are not universally identical to—a metal-insulator transition.

## Observable signatures

- **高自旋倾向：** Hund exchange favors occupying several orbitals with parallel spins when crystal-field splittings are small relative to multiplet energy gains.
- **低自旋倾向：** Increasing ligand-field splitting can favor paired occupation of lower orbitals, reducing the moment; pressure commonly changes both ligand geometry and hybridization.
- **巡游化倾向：** Pressure also broadens bands and strengthens hybridization, increasing charge fluctuations and potentially destroying identifiable local moments independently of a simple level crossing.
- **耦合转变：** Spin, volume, structure, and conductivity may change together because their free energies are coupled, but coincidence does not prove one is the sole cause of the others.
- **模型要求：** Retain the relevant orbital degeneracies, U, J, crystal-field matrix, bandwidths, structure, temperature, and entropy; a scalar U/W criterion omits the spin-state degree of freedom.
- **validation boundary：** Establish pressure-dependent structure and compare spin correlations, orbital occupations, spectra, and transport; agreement with one transition pressure is insufficient to validate the microscopic competition quantitatively.

## Conditions and boundaries
