## Core statement

掺杂引起的谱权重转移（doping-induced spectral-weight transfer）的核心陈述是：Doping a correlated insulator can transfer spectral weight between low-energy quasiparticle states and high-energy Hubbard or charge-transfer features, so the electronic structure changes beyond a rigid chemical-potential shift.

## Observable signatures

- **与刚性能带的对比：** In a rigid-band picture, dispersions and integrated band weights remain nearly fixed while the Fermi level moves; strong local interactions instead make available addition and removal states depend on occupancy.
- **机制：** Changing local charge probabilities redistributes matrix-element weight among many-body transitions and can pull weight across an energy scale comparable to the interaction-induced gap.
- **观测特征：** New in-gap or near-Fermi-level weight appears while high-energy sideband weight changes nonlinearly with carrier concentration; the total spectral sum rule remains constrained even though chosen band partitions need not.
- **电荷转移体系：** With ligand-metal covalency, doped holes may reside substantially on ligand-like states, so orbital-resolved transfer is basis dependent and should be assessed in an explicit p-d or consistently downfolded model.
- **必要对照：** Compare spectra at matched structure and temperature and assess disorder, chemical substitution, phase separation, and matrix-element changes before calling the transfer intrinsic.
- **边界：** Gap filling, gap closing, and chemical-potential motion are distinct behaviors, and spectral-weight transfer alone does not specify the pairing, transport, or magnetic mechanism of the doped state.
