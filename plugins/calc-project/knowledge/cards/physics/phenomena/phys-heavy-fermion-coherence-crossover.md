## Core statement

重费米子相干交叉（heavy-fermion coherence crossover）的核心陈述是：A heavy-fermion coherence crossover occurs below a material-specific scale when incoherent local f degrees of freedom acquire long-lived hybridized quasiparticle character, producing extremely narrow bands and a Fermi surface that counts the f electrons.

## Observable signatures

- **高温区间：** Above the coherence scale, local f moments are thermally disordered and their spectral weight near the Fermi energy is broad or incoherent, so conduction bands can resemble a small-Fermi-surface description.
- **低温区间：** Below the scale, Kondo screening and lattice coherence create a narrow f-derived resonance with strong conduction-f hybridization and a large effective mass.
- **费米面后果：** When quasiparticles are well defined at sufficiently low temperature, the Fermi volume includes the f electron count; a finite-temperature crossover need not define a sharp Fermi-surface volume.
- **可观测量：** Temperature-dependent momentum-resolved spectra, self-energy coherence, quasiparticle residue, transport, and thermodynamics should change consistently near the same scale.
- **interpretation boundary：** A narrow band alone does not prove Kondo-lattice coherence; compare orbital character, temperature evolution, scattering rate, and possible crystal-field or ordered-state explanations.
- **dmft boundary：** Single-site DMFT can describe local screening and coherence but omits nonlocal magnetic correlations that may compete near quantum critical regimes.
