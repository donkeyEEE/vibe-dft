# PRL main-text and supplement evidence allocation

Keep the main PRL text focused on the minimum evidence needed to establish the central claim, while placing reproducibility detail, extended datasets, and controls in supplementary material with clear main-text pointers.

## Use

Reserve main-text figures for the claim-critical comparison and its interpretation. Move material-growth characterization, repeated traces, extended parameter sweeps, and derivational detail to the supplement only when the main text still states what the supplemental evidence checks and where to find it.

## Caveats

Supplementary material cannot repair a missing logical link in the main argument. This card describes an observed allocation strategy; final placement must follow APS policy and the paper's specific evidentiary burden.
