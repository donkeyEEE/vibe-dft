# PRL figure prerequisite–signature–consequence order

For a multi-figure PRL argument, order the main evidence so that material or model prerequisites precede the decisive signature, which in turn precedes the scientific or functional consequence.

## Use

Use this as an evidence-logic check when arranging figures and results: the reader should understand the sample, symmetry, baseline, or model before being asked to interpret its signature. A later figure should use the signature to support a consequence that the earlier evidence has made credible. Where a conclusion depends on a tuning variable, the sequence should also expose the relevant parameter range or phase boundary.

## Caveats

This is not a universal chronological rule. A paper may lead with its headline result when the prerequisite can be supplied in the same figure or a short opening paragraph. Panel order must still follow the specific claim and data dependencies.

## Evidence

3QNRLT5F progresses from crystal/magnetic structure to SOC-dependent electronic structure and surface response (Figs. 1, 2, and 4; pp. 2–4). LX2FK5RU progresses from structure and transport to the phase diagram and Hall constraint (Figs. 1–3). SSXWMSWP progresses from baseline transition behavior to fluence-dependent loops and extracted metrics (Figs. 1–3). Batch-02 papers use recurrence, topology, and interaction phase maps to show the parameter dependence of their theoretical signatures.
