# PRL prediction condition–observable bridge

State a theoretical prediction as a conditional chain—material or model condition, calculated signature, and experimentally observable consequence—rather than as an unconditional mechanism claim.

## Use

Name the symmetry, tuning variable, filling, interaction regime, or structural assumption that enables the prediction. Then show the calculated signature and explain which measurement, response, or device behavior would distinguish it. This pattern makes a prediction auditable without overstating its experimental status.

## Caveats

An experimentally accessible consequence is not experimental confirmation. Keep proposed observables separate from data already measured.

## Evidence
