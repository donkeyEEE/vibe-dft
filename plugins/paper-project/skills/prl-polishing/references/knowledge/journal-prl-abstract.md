# PRL abstract requirements

A PRL abstract must be one self-contained paragraph of no more than 600 characters and must concisely state the subject, result, and conclusion without displayed equations, tables, or numbered references.

## Requirements

For experimental work, name the measured quantities, studied objects, and relevant experimental conditions. Avoid unexplained acronyms and coined terminology. If a source must appear in the abstract, write the source information directly rather than using a numbered citation. PRL Comments and Replies do not require abstracts.

## Caveats

The 600-character rule is PRL-specific; the general Physical Review guidance of roughly 5% of article length and fewer than 500 words does not replace it.
