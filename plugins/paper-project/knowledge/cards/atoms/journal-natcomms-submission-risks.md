# Nature Communications submission risks

Unbudgeted Methods length, vague reporting summaries, invalid availability identifiers, and generic cover letters are recurring Nature Communications desk-rejection or production-hold risks.

Initial submission normally uses one PDF with embedded figures; revisions may use the Springer Nature Word template or LaTeX `sn-jnl` with the `nature` option and `sn-nature.bst`. The journal is fully open access, transparent peer review is the default but can be deliberately declined, and transferred Nature manuscripts retain reviews while formatting is handled at revision. Verify all current policies before submission.
