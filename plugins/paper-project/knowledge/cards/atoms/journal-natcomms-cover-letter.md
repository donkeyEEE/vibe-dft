# Nature Communications cover letter

A Nature Communications cover letter should state the finding, its novelty, and its cross-disciplinary significance explicitly rather than relying on generic claims of broad interest.

Use three concise sentences: what was found, what is new, and why the result matters across disciplines. The manuscript cover page should also use an informative concise title, author and affiliation information, corresponding-author contact details, and applicable ORCID identifiers.
