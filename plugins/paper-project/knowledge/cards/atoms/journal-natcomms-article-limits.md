# Nature Communications article limits

For a Nature Communications Article, the approximately 5,000-word main-text limit includes Methods, so the word budget must reserve Methods space from the start.

Typical limits are approximately 5,000 words, a 150-word abstract, about 60 references, and up to 10 main-text display items. Brief Communications are approximately 2,000 words with 100-word abstracts; Reviews are approximately 6,000 words with 200-word abstracts; Perspectives are approximately 4,000 words with 150-word abstracts; Correspondence is approximately 500 words with no abstract. Verify current journal guidance before submission.
