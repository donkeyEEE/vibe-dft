# PRL title style

A PRL title should be self-contained, simple, concise, and informative, avoid nonstandard abbreviations and unnecessary opening articles, and use APS title-style capitalization.

## Requirements

For PRL, capitalize the first letter of each principal word while normally leaving conjunctions, prepositions, and articles lowercase unless they follow a colon or dash. Do not use evaluative words such as “important” or “precise” as substitutes for a concrete finding.
