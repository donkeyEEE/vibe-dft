# PRL run-in headings

PRL generally avoids freestanding section headings and instead uses run-in headings set at the start of an indented paragraph.

## Style

The primary PRL heading is italic, begins with a capital letter, and is followed by an em dash before the paragraph text. A further level may use a roman heading followed by a colon. Additional subtopics may use an italic sentence-style phrase followed by a period.

## Caveats

Headings are not mandatory. Use them only when they clarify the argument of a short Letter, and verify the manuscript preparation route against current APS production guidance.
