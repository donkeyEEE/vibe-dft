# AI writing boundaries

AI may improve presentation under author verification, but it must not supply unchecked scientific claims, references, data, or concealed substantive images.

Grammar, clarity, outline options, title variants, and checked translations are generally acceptable. Method or result explanations, reviewer-response frameworks, and code or statistics explanations require strong human control and reproducibility checks. Do not ask AI to fabricate a paper’s core argument, insert unverified content, expose unpublished or peer-review material to public models, or conceal substantive image creation.
