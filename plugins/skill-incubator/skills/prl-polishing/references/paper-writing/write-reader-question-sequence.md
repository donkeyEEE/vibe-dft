# Reader question sequence

A scientific manuscript should help readers resolve relevance, novelty, trust, reuse, and meaning in that order.

## Sequence

1. **Relevance** — Is this for me?
2. **Novelty** — What is new here?
3. **Trust** — Do I believe it?
4. **Reuse** — Can I use it?
5. **Meaning** — What does it mean, and where are the boundaries?

Do not bury novelty under methods detail, and do not polish sentence fluency while leaving relevance or boundaries invisible.
