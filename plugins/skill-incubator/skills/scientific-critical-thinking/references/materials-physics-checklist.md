# Materials Physics Critical-Evaluation Checklist

## First-principles and DFT

Check structural model, stoichiometry, vacancies/dopants, dimensionality, and relaxation criteria. Record exchange-correlation functional, PAW/pseudopotential, Hubbard `U`, SOC, vdW treatment, cutoff, k-mesh, smearing, supercell size, and convergence evidence.

For magnetic claims, compare plausible magnetic configurations and state the mapping from total energies to exchange parameters. Treat a Curie temperature as model-dependent: identify the spin model, anisotropy, interaction range, dimensionality, and Monte Carlo/mean-field approximation. Do not equate a phonon calculation with full finite-temperature or chemical stability.

**Overclaim flags:** one magnetic order tested; no convergence data; unexplained `U`; bulk calculation used for a monolayer claim; model Tc presented as an experimental prediction without uncertainty.

## Magnetism and Spectroscopy

Check composition, thickness, substrate, disorder, oxidation, and magnetic-field/temperature geometry. For magnetometry, inspect background subtraction, diamagnetic substrate correction, normalization, hysteresis reproducibility, and phase impurity controls. For XMCD, ARPES, transport, diffraction, scattering, and microscopy, ask whether the observable directly identifies the proposed microscopic mechanism or only correlates with it.

**Overclaim flags:** ferromagnetic signal attributed to the target phase without impurity controls; spectroscopic feature treated as unique evidence of a mechanism; a field-dependent signal treated as proof of intrinsic long-range order.

## Synthesis and Characterization

Check phase purity, stoichiometry, elemental homogeneity, crystallographic refinement, sample-to-sample reproducibility, and whether controls match the claimed variable. Separate evidence for successful synthesis from evidence for electronic or magnetic mechanism.

**Overclaim flags:** nominal composition treated as measured composition; one characterization method used to establish several unrelated claims; no comparison sample for doping, strain, thickness, or processing effects.

## Theory–Experiment Comparison

Verify that theory and experiment describe the same material state: composition, site occupancy, defects, thickness, strain, substrate, temperature, field, and magnetic configuration. Evaluate trend agreement separately from absolute-value agreement. List alternative mechanisms and decide whether the evidence excludes them.

**Overclaim flags:** room-temperature experimental behavior explained by zero-temperature pristine-cell calculation alone; calculated and measured values compared without matching conditions; agreement in one observable presented as validation of a complete mechanism.
