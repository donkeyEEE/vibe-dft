## Core statement

关联驱动能隙的磁序独立性（order independence of a correlation-driven gap）的核心陈述是：A practical signature of a correlation-driven insulating state is that its gap or pronounced Fermi-level spectral depletion persists when long-range magnetic order is removed, whereas a Slater-like gap collapses with the order that folds or spin-splits the bands.

## Definition and distinctions

- **受控比较：** Compare ordered and symmetry-unbroken paramagnetic states at matched structure, composition, interaction parameters, and temperature as closely as the methods allow.
- **直接观测量：** Use single-particle spectra and activated or insulating transport rather than relying only on ordered magnetic moments or a static mean-field band gap.
- **有限温边界：** At nonzero temperature conductivity is not strictly zero and spectral gaps can be thermally filled, so report activation scales or Fermi-level weight instead of an absolute metal-insulator label alone.
- **结构混杂因素：** If the magnetic transition coincides with structural distortion, charge order, or orbital order, gap persistence cannot be assigned solely to local correlations without separating those changes.
- **方法边界：** A paramagnetic calculation that suppresses order but also changes the approximation, entropy, or disorder model is not a perfectly controlled removal of magnetism.
- **conclusion boundary：** Order independence supports correlation-driven insulation but does not identify a unique Mott-Hubbard, charge-transfer, or orbital-selective mechanism.
