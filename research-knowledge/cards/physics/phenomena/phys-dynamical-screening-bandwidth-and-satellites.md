## Core statement

动态屏蔽导致的带宽重整化与卫星峰（dynamical-screening bandwidth renormalization and satellites）的核心陈述是：Dynamical charge screening has two linked spectral consequences: it reduces the coherent low-energy quasiparticle weight and bandwidth and transfers the missing weight into high-energy satellites associated with screening excitations.

## Observable signatures

- **玻色表示：** Represent the frequency dependence of U by coupling local charge to bosonic screening modes such as plasmons; integrating out the bosons produces a retarded density interaction.
- **静态约化：** At energies far below a screening mode, relaxation of that mode lowers the effective static repulsion relative to the bare interaction.
- **相干重整化：** Dressing an electron with the screening cloud reduces its overlap with a bare electron, narrowing the low-energy band by a bosonic factor even before additional Hubbard correlations are considered.
- **卫星峰：** Creating or removing an electron together with one or more screening excitations produces sidebands separated by characteristic boson energies and carrying the spectral weight lost from the coherent peak.
- **电荷与自旋的区别：** Charge screening strongly renormalizes monopole U, whereas neutral intra-atomic multiplet and Hund splittings are often less screened; this separation is approximate and orbital dependent.
- **边界：** A static U chosen to reproduce the low-energy interaction does not automatically reproduce the bandwidth renormalization or satellites, and a satellite energy alone does not uniquely identify the screening mode.
