# PRL model-to-validation pairing

When a PRL claim relies on a model or mechanism, present the model-derived signature beside a validation layer that tests the same signature in calculation or measurement, and state the conditions over which that correspondence remains valid.

## Use

Make the visual and textual correspondence explicit: identify the shared observable, the changed condition, and what agreement or mismatch would mean. Use a parameter scan, phase boundary, or comparison calculation when it is needed to distinguish a robust prediction from a special-point result.

## Caveats

The validation layer must be methodologically independent enough to test the claim; repeating a transformed version of the same input is not sufficient. The card does not prescribe a two-panel layout.

## Evidence

EL4F62IM aligns minimal-model spectral-function maps with DMFT maps (Figs. 1 and 3, pp. 3 and 5). 3QNRLT5F develops a symmetry-and-band signature toward an observable magnetoelectric consequence (pp. 2–4). FCVJIIYX uses angular decomposition and switching data to test a nonconventional torque interpretation (pp. 3–5). The batch-02 theory papers test their conclusions against twist-angle, band, symmetry, filling, or interaction conditions (26XQIYJF, 3XSHZEGH, DRAH3Z2W, F2QKJA9G, and KRCYBBYR).
