# Nature Communications references

Nature Communications uses sequential superscript Nature-style references, with article numbers rather than page ranges for online-only journal articles.

Use abbreviated ISO 4 journal names, `Last, Initials` author formatting, `&` before the final author, bold volume numbers, and years in parentheses. References appear before Methods in the published article; DOIs are encouraged. Verify current style guidance before submission.
