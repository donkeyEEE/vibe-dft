# Physical Review Supplemental Material boundary

Physical Review Supplemental Material may contain useful supporting information, but the article must remain understandable and convincing without it.

## Allowed role

Suitable material includes multimedia, calculation parameters, sample-preparation details, and equation derivations. The main article must cite the Supplemental Material with a brief description, and references cited in the supplement must also appear in the main article’s reference list.

## Boundary

Supplemental Material must not be used merely to evade a length limit or to carry evidence essential to the main conclusion. APS editors may require a longer article with the material integrated into the main text. Comments, Replies, and Errata generally should not have Supplemental Material.
