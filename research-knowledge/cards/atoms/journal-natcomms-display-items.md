# Nature Communications display items

Nature Communications permits at most 10 combined main-text figures and tables, with additional peer-reviewed material placed in Supplementary Information.

Use 1,200 dpi for line art, 300 dpi for halftones, and 600 dpi for combination art; accepted formats include TIFF, EPS, PDF, and JPEG. Design in RGB for online publication, use 89 mm single-column or 183 mm double-column widths, 5–7 pt sans-serif in-figure fonts, and lowercase bold panel labels. Dense multi-panel figures are accepted only while readable.
