# PRL effective-model assumption boundary

When an argument uses an effective, projected, or symmetry-reduced model, declare the assumptions that define it and identify which conclusions are robust within those assumptions versus conditional on them.

## Use

Report the retained degrees of freedom, neglected terms, symmetry or parameter regime, and the diagnostic used to test robustness. Put indispensable assumptions in the main argument; detailed derivations may move to supplementary material.

## Caveats

Listing assumptions is not a robustness analysis. Do not claim universality when a conclusion has only been evaluated at a special parameter value or in one projected basis.

## Evidence

26XQIYJF compares continuum-model conditions and gap evolution (pp. 1–6). KRCYBBYR makes the Wannier-basis projection and its approximations explicit (pp. 1–4). DRAH3Z2W identifies the restricted flat-band model and competing phases (pp. 1–4). XXP78P2F directly demonstrates that correlated-orbital construction can change quantitative DFT+DMFT conclusions (pp. 1–14).
