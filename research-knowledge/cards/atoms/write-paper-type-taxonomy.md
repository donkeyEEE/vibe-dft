# Paper-type taxonomy

Classify a manuscript by its dominant argument structure before applying section-level writing guidance.

| Type | Central reader question |
|---|---|
| `research` | What was found and what does it mean? |
| `methods` | Does it work, improve on alternatives, and reproduce? |
| `hypothesis` | Is the proposed mechanism the right explanation? |
| `algorithmic` | Does the procedure, model, system, or device perform under fair comparison, and where does it fail? |
| `review` | What is known, disputed, and unresolved? |

Use the user’s stated framing first. A mechanism-centered experiment is normally `hypothesis`; a validated protocol is `methods`; a new model or device with comparative performance is `algorithmic`; literature synthesis without new primary data is `review`; otherwise use `research`.
