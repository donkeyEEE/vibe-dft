# Physical Review figure preparation

Physical Review figures must remain scientifically accurate and readable at final journal size, with sequential numbering, one explanatory caption, defined symbols and curves, described subpanels, labeled quantities and units, and accessible color choices.

## Requirements

Number figures in order of first reference and label subpanels `(a)`, `(b)`, and so on. Define each panel and symbol in the caption or legend. Label axes with the plotted quantity and put units in parentheses. At final size, the smallest capital letters and numerals should be at least 2 mm high, data points at least 1 mm in diameter, and curve widths at least 0.18 mm. Disclose image adjustments such as brightness changes in the caption. Figures intended to work in grayscale must remain distinguishable without color alone.

## Caveats

File-format and production requirements can change. Verify current APS figure instructions before final export.
