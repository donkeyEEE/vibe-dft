# PRL quantitative claim with criterion

Whenever a PRL figure supports a quantitative claim, state the operational criterion, the comparison condition, and the displayed uncertainty or variability needed to interpret that claim.

## Use

Apply this to transition temperatures, switching thresholds, fitted quantities, phase boundaries, and extracted metrics. The criterion belongs in the caption, methods, or main text near the figure; it must let a reader distinguish an observed value from a visual impression.

## Caveats

The appropriate uncertainty representation depends on the experiment or calculation. Do not add error bars where they have no defined meaning, but do not hide sample variation, fitting assumptions, or selection criteria behind a clean schematic.

## Evidence
