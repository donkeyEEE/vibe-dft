# Nature Communications abstract

A Nature Communications Article abstract is an unstructured paragraph of at most 150 words that leads with the finding rather than background.

Do not use citations. Spell out abbreviations on first use, quantify results where possible, and state concrete performance rather than vague significance. Verify the current guideline before final submission.
