# PRL Letter length budget

A Physical Review Letters Letter has a 3,750-word-equivalent limit, and APS calculates that total from body text, displayed mathematics, figures, and tables rather than prose alone.

## Counting rule

For Letters and other short papers, counted text includes the article body, figure and table captions, and footnotes or endnotes. The title, author and affiliation list, abstract, publication-history material, PhySH keywords, DOI, references, author byline footnotes, and acknowledgments are excluded from the text count. Displayed equations, figures, and tables contribute APS-defined word equivalents based on rows, dimensions, or table lines.

## Caveats

Do not treat an ordinary word-processor count as the final PRL length. Check the current APS length calculator and article-type table before submission because limits and conversion formulas may change.
