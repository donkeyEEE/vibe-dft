# Physical Review data availability statement

Physical Review submissions require authors to provide a data availability statement, which is published as a separate paragraph after the acknowledgments when the article is accepted.

## Requirements

APS strongly encourages authors to deposit supporting data, code, software, and related materials in open repositories and to follow funder requirements. When public data are cited, provide creators, year, version when applicable, repository name, and a persistent identifier, preferably a DOI. Authors may amend the statement during proofing.

## Caveats

The appropriate statement depends on whether data are open, available on request, restricted, or not applicable. Verify the current APS data-availability policy and target-journal instructions rather than reusing a generic sentence.
