# Cite borrowed intellectual content

Provide attribution for others’ ideas, data, methods, wording, structure, images, and distinctive interpretations.

During proofreading, verify missing citations alongside grammar, typographical errors, figure numbering, and whether a reader can follow the paper without unnecessary effort.
